## Lessons Learned

**Process**
1. Reading a template's rules and checklist before writing saves rework. From Sprint 2 on, every deliverable starts with its required items copied into the Jira work item as a Definition of Done.
2. A sprint plan is only realistic when it is sized against the hours members can actually give. We will estimate work items and compare the sprint scope with the 7-hours-per-week capacity at sprint planning.
3. Requirements are easier to manage when they are clarified at the start of the sprint. We will do the template walkthrough in the first team meeting of each sprint rather than close to the deadline.

**Development environment**
4. Each artifact needs one source of truth. The Jira backlog is the source for work items, and the GitHub repository is the source for project documents, which then sync to Confluence; documents are not edited directly in Confluence.
5. Tool setup goes faster when one member owns each tool.

**Team collaboration**
6. Regular weekly meetings and decisions taken together worked well, and we will keep them.
7. Matching responsibilities to experience helped every member contribute. In Sprint 2 we will also pair members on tasks outside their experience, so that knowledge of the tools and the RAG pipeline is spread across the team.
