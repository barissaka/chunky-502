# Third-party Dataset Register

Project: Traffic rules and penalties assistant · Team: Chunky

The corpus of Chunky is the six legal texts below, used as published: the consolidated ("güncel") text of each one is downloaded as PDF from the Presidency Legislation Information System (mevzuat.gov.tr) and used without editing. Text extraction and article-level chunking are described in the [System Architecture Design Document](../11-system-architecture-design-document.md); the resulting chunk set and the question–answer set are documented in the [Dataset Report](02-dataset-report.md) and the [Q&A Data Report](03-qa-data-report.md), not here.

Notes

- **Version.** mevzuat.gov.tr serves the consolidated text as of the day it was last regenerated; the "Version" column is the creation date recorded in the downloaded PDF. If a text is amended during the project, the new consolidated text is downloaded, both dates are updated here and the index is rebuilt (see the design document, offline pipeline).
- **Licence.** Under Article 31 of the Law on Intellectual and Artistic Works (No. 5846), laws, regulations and other official texts are not protected works and may be reproduced and distributed freely. No licence terms apply to their use in this project.
- **Reference numbers** [1]–[6] match the References list of the [Project Executive Summary](../03-project-canvas/01-project-executive-summary.md).
- **Where the files are.** The six source PDFs are committed in `data/legal/pdf/` and the plain text extracted from them (one UTF-8 file per text, `pdftotext -layout`, footnotes and Official Gazette tables removed) in `data/legal/`, with the same numbering `01_kanun_2918` … `06_elektrikli_skuter`. The links above are the download source.
