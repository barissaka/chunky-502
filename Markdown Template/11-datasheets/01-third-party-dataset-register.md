# Third-party Dataset Register

Project: Traffic rules and penalties assistant · Team: Chunky

The corpus of Chunky is the six legal texts below, used as published. Each text was downloaded from the Presidency Legislation Information System (mevzuat.gov.tr) as the consolidated ("güncel") text. Following AD-09 of the design document (Word where mevzuat.gov.tr offers it, otherwise PDF), the format in use is **Word (.docx) for all six texts**; mevzuat.gov.tr offered a Word version of every text. How the files are processed is described in the [System Architecture Design Document](../11-system-architecture-design-document-local.md), section 5 Data pipeline; the chunk set and the question–answer set are documented in the [Dataset Report](02-dataset-report.md) and the [Q&A Data Report](03-qa-data-report.md), not here.

Notes

- **Version.** The "Version" column is the date of the latest amendment incorporated in the consolidated text: the Official Gazette (RG) date of the latest amending regulation, or for the Law the RG date of the latest amending law. It is read from the amendment notes in the text ("Değişik:RG-…", "Ek:…") and, where present, the amendment table at the end of the text. The document set is a snapshot taken on the date of access and is not updated during the project, even if a text is amended (charter constraint C-02). At the start of each sprint the last amendment date of each text on its official source is compared with the version here; a difference is reported as a limitation in the sprint report, not applied to the corpus (Major Activities rows 5 and 27).
- **Change of snapshot (02 Nov 2026).** Until 02 Nov 2026 the corpus was the PDF versions accessed by Barış Saka on 06 Oct 2026 (texts [1], [2]) and 11 Oct 2026 (texts [3]–[6]). When the corpus switched to the Word versions, the amendment notes of each text were compared with the PDF versions. Texts [1]–[5] contain the same amendments. **Text [6] differs:** the Word version includes the amendment of 13 Oct 2026 (RG 33399), which rewrote Article 22 (administrative fines; for example the 1.000 TL fine became 8.687 TL). The PDF version, accessed two days before that amendment, did not include it. Answers in the question set (D2) that rest on Article 22 of the Electric Scooter Regulation must be checked against the new text.
- **Format conversion.** Texts [1], [2], [5] and [6] were downloaded as legacy Word (.doc) files; texts [3] and [4] were served as HTML files with a .doc extension. All six were opened in Microsoft Word and saved as .docx without changes to the content. The download of text [3] had a character-encoding error (Turkish characters garbled); the encoding was reversed before saving, and the article headers of the repaired file were checked against the PDF version (74 of 74 identical).
- **Footnotes and notes.** The Law's 91 footnotes are kept by Word in a separate footnote area and are not part of the body text. In the regulations, explanatory notes are listed at the end of the text. Neither the footnotes nor the end notes are part of the corpus (see the design document, section 5).
- **Known gaps and remarks per text.**
  - [1] According to the Law's amendment table, the amendment of Article 103 by Law No. 7574 enters into force on 1 Jan 2027, after the live demo.
  - [3] The annexes are not in the text; mevzuat.gov.tr only links to them ("Ekleri için tıklayınız"). Articles 7 and 28 carry notes on a stay of execution by the Council of State (Danıştay) dated 8 Oct 2025.
  - [4] The amendment table at the end of the text lists only the amendment of 30 Sep 2021; the amendment notes in the text also show amendments of 29 Dec 2015 and 13 Jun 2020.
  - [5] The annexes are not in the text; mevzuat.gov.tr only links to them.
- **Licence.** Under Article 31 of the Law on Intellectual and Artistic Works (No. 5846), laws, regulations and other official texts are not protected works and may be reproduced and distributed freely. No licence terms apply to their use in this project.
- **Experiments.** D5 (long-context baseline) and D6 (RAG baseline) are the charter's deliverables; E1, E2, … are the experiment cards in `../12-experiment-cards/` that produce them.
- **Reference numbers** [1]–[6] match the References list of the [Project Executive Summary](../03-project-canvas/01-project-executive-summary.md).
- **Where the files are.** The six source files are committed in `data/legal/word/` as `01_kanun_2918.docx` … `06_elektrikli_skuter.docx`. The clean text of each document is produced by `src/chunky/extract.py` into `data/clean/` with the same numbering and checked by `notebooks/02_extract_check.ipynb`. The machine-readable copy of this register, from which the chunker takes each chunk's document metadata, is `data/legal/documents.yaml`; it must be updated together with this page. The earlier PDF snapshot stays in `data/legal/pdf/`; the plain-text files in `data/legal/*.txt` and `src/check_articles.py` belong to the earlier PDF pipeline and are no longer used. The links above are the download source.
