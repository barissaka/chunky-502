# Third-party Dataset Register

Project: Traffic rules and penalties assistant · Team: Chunky

The corpus of Chunky is the six legal texts below, used as published. Each text was downloaded from the Presidency Legislation Information System (mevzuat.gov.tr) as the consolidated ("güncel") text; the format in use is PDF for all six (AD-09 of the design document: the Word version is adopted where mevzuat.gov.tr offers it, and this register is updated for any text that switches). How the files are processed is described in the [System Architecture Design Document](../11-system-architecture-design-document.md), section 5 Data pipeline; the chunk set and the question–answer set are documented in the [Dataset Report](02-dataset-report.md) and the [Q&A Data Report](03-qa-data-report.md), not here.

Notes

- **Version.** mevzuat.gov.tr serves the consolidated text as of the day it was last regenerated; the "Version" column is the creation date recorded in the downloaded file. The document set is a snapshot taken on the dates of access and is not updated during the project, even if a text is amended (charter constraint C-02). At the start of each sprint the last amendment date of each text on its official source is compared with the version here; a difference is reported as a limitation in the sprint report, not applied to the corpus (Major Activities rows 5 and 27).
- **Licence.** Under Article 31 of the Law on Intellectual and Artistic Works (No. 5846), laws, regulations and other official texts are not protected works and may be reproduced and distributed freely. No licence terms apply to their use in this project.
- **Experiments.** D5 (long-context baseline) and D6 (RAG baseline) are the charter's deliverables; E1, E2, … are the experiment cards in `../12-experiment-cards/` that produce them.
- **Reference numbers** [1]–[6] match the References list of the [Project Executive Summary](../03-project-canvas/01-project-executive-summary.md).
- **Where the files are.** The six source files are committed in `data/legal/pdf/` and their plain-text versions in `data/legal/`, numbered `01_kanun_2918` … `06_elektrikli_skuter`; the plain text is verified against the source files by `src/check_articles.py` (`docs/eval/article-check-2026-10-25.md`). The links above are the download source.
